# Ambiguities and explicit interpretations

1. **Event day versus value day.** E7 arrives on D5 but has value day D2; E9 arrives on D6 and also has value day D2. Each entry stores both. A replay row for D5 includes E7 but cannot include E9, which is not known until D6. The D6 row includes E9. This is an event-time view, not a retroactively restated historical report.
2. **Meaning of the D2 balance criterion.** The phrase “evaluated at end of Day 5 and before any fee is assessed” is interpreted as a D5-known, value-dated D2 balance. It is AED 1,200 − AED 950 − AED 620 = −AED 370.
3. **Backdated fee timing.** When E7 arrives on D5, the engine evaluates value-day balances through D5 using events known by D5, then assesses one AED 25 fee per negative account/value day. The negative days in this scenario are D2, D4, and D5. Fees are append-only and do not disappear when E9 reverses E7's principal.
4. **Fee impact on later days.** A fee booked to a value day affects subsequent value-day balances. The fee is assessed based on the negative balance before adding that day's fee, avoiding a circular test.
5. **Authorization holds.** A hold changes available balance, not ledger balance. Auth-A is approved because AED 250 − AED 200 remains non-negative, then its AED 185 settlement is accepted on D4 and the unused hold is released. Auth-B is checked against the D5 ledger balance after known fees; its AED 90 hold would make available balance negative, so it is rejected and no active hold is applied.
6. **Settlement accounting.** E5 posts an AED 185 debit against Auth-A's AED 200 hold. This exercise treats settlement as consuming the hold and releasing the unused AED 15. Partial settlement policy beyond this scenario is unspecified.
7. **Interest timing.** Interest is accrued on each day's positive event-time closing balance, rounded to currency precision per day, and accumulated off-ledger. One credit is posted on D6 for the sum of those rounded accruals. The exercise does not explicitly say whether a later backdated event should trigger retroactive interest recalculation; this implementation does not rewrite earlier event-time accruals and does not compound interest daily.
8. **Remainder policy.** The rounded daily accruals are summed exactly into the D6 capitalized amount. No rounding remainder is discarded. The BHD instalment split separately allocates its rounding remainder to the first instalment.
9. **Reversal semantics.** E9 appends a compensating credit linked to E7. It does not mutate or delete E7, and it does not automatically reverse fees assessed earlier.
10. **Errors.** E6 is rejected because Auth-Z does not exist; no settlement entry is appended. A rejected Auth-B authorization is recorded as a state and error, not as a hold or ledger entry.
11. **Scope.** This is a compact deterministic exercise implementation, not a general ledger. Concurrency, persistence, idempotency storage, account locking, external event parsing, and regulatory controls are outside scope.
